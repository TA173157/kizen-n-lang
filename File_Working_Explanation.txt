============================================================
High-Level Pipeline (How these files work together)
============================================================
1. `Lexer` scans source text and emits tokens (`Token.h`, `Lexer.h`, `Lexer.cpp`).
2. `Parser` consumes tokens and builds AST nodes (`parser.h`, `parser.cpp`, `AST.h`).
3. `SemanticAnalyzer` visits AST to enforce type/scope/validity rules (`SemanticAnalyzer.h`, `SemanticAnalyzer.cpp`).
4. `IRGenerator` visits AST and emits LLVM IR (`IRGenerator.h`, `IRGenerator.cpp`).

This is the full front-end + semantic stage + LLVM IR backend flow for your language.
